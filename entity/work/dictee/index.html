Theresa Hak Kyung Cha, Dictee. New York: Tanam Press, 1982. 179 pp. Artists' book structured around nine chapters named after the Greek Muses, preceded by a prologue centered on the figure of the diseuse (female monologue performer). The work draws on autobiography, history, film, and religious text across multiple languages including Korean, English, French, and Latin.
