Untranslatability

번역불가능성

ConceptType

URI https://kumji.github.io/dictee-graph/entity/concept/untranslatability · JSON-LD

The remainder that resists full transfer between languages. In Dictee, untranslatability works as a textual strategy rather than a failure: French and English are set on facing pages without equivalence, so that homophones such as cygnes/signes and tense distinctions such as the imperfect j'écoutais against the simple past "I heard" leave a political residue that neither language can absorb. Distinct from dict:concept/translational-violence, which names the loss imposed by colonial language policy; untranslatability names what the text withholds in response. Draws on Emily Apter, Against World Literature: On the Politics of Untranslatability (2013), and Gayatri Chakravorty Spivak, "The Politics of Translation" (1993).
