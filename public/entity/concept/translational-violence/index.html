Epistemic violence enacted through forced linguistic assimilation across generations. Draws on Spivak's concept of epistemic violence and Fanon's analysis of colonial internalization, applied to the intergenerational linguistic displacement experienced by Cha and her mother under colonial and diasporic conditions.
